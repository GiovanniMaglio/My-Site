About

Giovanni Maglio

I'm a physics graduate from the University of Milan, currently preparing applications for a PhD in theoretical physics.

My research interests centre on quantum field theory, gauge symmetries and the mathematical structure of the Standard Model. I am also drawn to questions at the frontier between particle physics and quantum gravity — string theory and its connections to low-energy effective field theories.

Alongside physics I care about literature, philosophy of science and science communication. I believe that clarity of thought and clarity of writing go hand in hand.
