Thesis

B.Sc. Thesis · University of Milan · 2025

Study of Drell–Yan Processes in Neutral and Charged Current with Variations of Electroweak Parameters

This thesis examines how variations in the electroweak parameters affect the structure of the electroweak Lagrangian, and how those changes propagate to the Drell–Yan cross sections in both the neutral-current and charged-current channels.
